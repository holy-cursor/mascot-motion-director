# Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming, professional collaboration space. Be respectful of contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Give constructive, specific feedback; assume good faith; credit creative work; and avoid harassment, personal attacks, sexualized behavior, intimidation, and publishing private information.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or limit participation when these expectations are violated. For ordinary concerns use GitHub's private maintainer contact features. For security concerns use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to repository discussions, pull requests, issue threads, and project-associated community spaces.
